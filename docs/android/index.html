Test HappyLeafy on Android 🌿

Get the app from Google Play before it's released. It takes two taps and about a minute.

Do this on your Android phone, signed in with the Google account you use for the Play Store.

Step 1: Join the testers group

Opens Google Groups. Tap Join group. You won't get emails from it.

Join the testers group

Step 2: Become a tester and install

Tap Become a tester, then download it on Google Play and Install.

Get HappyLeafy on Google Play

Please keep it for 14 days 🙏

Google only lets a new app go public after at least 12 testers have kept it installed for 14 days in a row. Please leave it on your phone (and stay in the test) for two weeks, even if you don't open it every day.

Good to know

  • "App not found" in step 2? Wait a few minutes after step 1 and try again.
  • Several Google accounts on your phone? In the Play Store, switch to the one you used in step 1 (tap your picture, top right).
  • Feedback, bugs, ideas: info@happyleafy.com. Screenshots are great.
  • For adults (18+). See the privacy policy.
  • On iPhone? Try the web version instead.
