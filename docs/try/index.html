Help test HappyLeafy 🌿

Thanks for trying it! It takes 2 minutes to set up, and there's nothing to install from the App Store.

Open HappyLeafy →

1. Put it on your home screen

iPhone (Safari)
  1. Tap Open HappyLeafy above.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow ↑) at the bottom.
  3. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open HappyLeafy from the new icon on your home screen.
Android (Chrome)
  1. Tap Open HappyLeafy above.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

2. Things to try

  1. Add 2–3 of your plants (tap Identify and take a photo of a leaf).
  2. Open a plant and look at its care guide, watering amount and schedule.
  3. Tap Ask AI on a plant and ask a question.
  4. If a plant looks unwell, try Doctor with a close-up photo.
  5. Mark something as watered on Today.

3. Tell us what you think

What was confusing? What did you like? What's missing? Email info@happyleafy.com. Screenshots are great.

Good to know

  • This test version has no reminder notifications and no light meter (those come with the App Store version).
  • Add it to your home screen first, then add your plants from the home-screen icon. On iPhone the icon and Safari keep separate data, and Safari may clear data for sites you haven't opened in a while.
  • Your plants are saved on your phone only. To move them later, use Settings → Backup.
  • Each phone gets a free daily allowance of plant identifications and AI questions.
  • For adults (18+). Please don't put personal information in photos or AI questions. See the privacy policy.
