Ready? Set it up in 2 minutes

1. Open it and add it to your home screen first

Open HappyLeafy

iPhone (Safari): tap Share (square with an arrow ↑) → Add to Home Screen → Add.

Android (Chrome): tap ⋮ → Install app or Add to Home screen.

Do this before adding plants and always open it from the home-screen icon. On iPhone the icon and Safari keep separate data.

2. Add 2–3 of your plants

Tap Identify and photograph a leaf, or Add one by name. Then open a plant to see its care guide and water amount.

3. Try the advice

Tap Ask AI on a plant, or use Doctor with a close-up of anything that looks unwell. Mark something as watered on Today.

Good to know

  • Your plants are saved on your phone only. Use Settings → Backup to keep a copy.
  • Each phone gets a free daily allowance of plant identifications and AI questions.
  • For adults (18+). Photos and questions you send for identification or AI advice go to Pl@ntNet and Google; please don't include personal information. See the privacy policy.
